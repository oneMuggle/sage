Q3 Growth Slowed Because Trial Conversion Collapsed

Growth Team

Quarterly business review · October 2026

Three things happened this quarter, and only one of them is permanent

  • Signup volume grew 22% — acquisition is not the problem
  • Trial-to-paid conversion fell from 34% to 19%
  • The billing migration shipped in week 6 is the cause

Trial conversion fell 15 points in the six weeks after the billing migration

  • Drop is concentrated in self-serve signups, not sales-assisted
  • Error rate on the upgrade step tripled on the day of launch
  • Recovered partially after a hotfix, but never to baseline
19% trial→paid conversion, Q3 average
down from 34% in Q2

Reverting the upgrade step recovers two thirds of the loss

  • A/B test ran Sep 8 – Sep 22 on 12,400 trials
  • Legacy upgrade path: 27% conversion, significant at p < 0.01
  • Shipping the revert for all self-serve accounts this sprint

Approve the billing revert and we return to 30%+ next quarter
